# Receipt Sorter — Idea Council memo
Fictional founder · mode: standard

## Recommendation
**Test a $9/month "Receipt Sorter" for sole traders**: photograph receipts all month, get one sorted, totalled file at month end, before building anything beyond a one-day prototype.

## Why (three reasons, each with evidence)
1. **Pain plus money already spent.** `[invented]` "I lose a Sunday every month sorting receipts into a spreadsheet." `[invented]` "I pay my bookkeeper £40 just to type them in." Eleven independent voices across two source families, four with a stated spend.
2. **A specific wedge, not "cheaper".** Existing apps are built for accountants; sole traders say they only need a monthly file their accountant accepts. The wedge is the one-file handoff.
3. **Fits the founder.** A photo inbox, category rules and a CSV/PDF export are about two weeks for one person; no sales calls; discovery through "receipt app for freelancers" searches.

## Size (range, assumptions stated)
Reachable buyers per year: **2k low / 8k base / 25k high** (assumption). At $9/month, $1k MRR needs about 111 subscribers: 1.4% of the base pool.

## What would make us wrong
- A free feature in an existing accounting app may already do this (to verify).
- The evidence is two source families; a third, such as freelancer forums, would raise confidence.
- Spreadsheet users may not leave a tool they own and trust (the riskiest assumption).

## Next 7 days (pre-registered)
- **Test 0, wedge check (1 day):** try three existing apps with 30 real receipts. **Kill if** any exports a clean monthly file with no manual fixing.
- **Test 1, desirability:** message 100 sole traders who posted about receipt pain, with one honest question and an offer of early access. **Pass:** at least 5 replies reaching a call or waitlist email and at least 1 refundable pre-payment. **Kill:** under 5% replies or zero pre-payments.

## Confidence
**Medium.** Strong, specific pain with spend; but two source families, an unverified wedge and a sized-by-assumption market.
